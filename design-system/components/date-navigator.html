Date navigator

The visible week is always Monday–Sunday and never reflows when you tap a day — only the arrows change which week is shown. Any past date can be opened and edited freely, with no marking that an entry was made late. The strip distinguishes recorded from unknown at a glance — unknown is a state, not a zero.

Dashed border, no dot = unknown. Solid + dot = recorded. Filled accent = the day you're viewing. Accent ring = today. Dimmed = not reached yet, can't be selected. Arrows step a full week; the right arrow disables once the visible week reaches today.

When the visible week crosses a month (or year) boundary, the header names both: Month1 - Month2 Year, or Month1 Year1 - Month2 Year2 if the year changes too.
